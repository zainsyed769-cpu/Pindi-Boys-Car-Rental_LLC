# Terms & Conditions — Lease-to-Own Program

**Prime Hire Car Rental**, Dubai, UAE · Fleet operated under Pindi Boys Car Rental L.L.C

> Customer-facing summary. Read together with the signed Lease-to-Own Agreement,
> which prevails in case of conflict. All figures shown in calculators/quotes
> are **indicative** and subject to credit approval and vehicle availability.

## Eligibility

- Minimum age **21**, with a valid **UAE driving licence** and **Emirates ID**.
- Proof of income: salary certificate **or** business trade licence.
- **3–6 months** bank statements.
- Satisfactory credit / AECB check.

## What you pay

- A **down payment** (0%–40% of the vehicle value — higher down = lower monthly).
- A **fixed monthly payment** that includes finance, **comprehensive insurance**,
  **scheduled service**, **registration renewal**, and **5% VAT**.
- A **refundable security deposit** (returned at the end, less any dues).

## What is NOT included

Fuel · Salik/tolls · traffic fines · parking · Salik recharges · damage beyond
fair wear & tear · insurance excess on at-fault claims.

## Terms available

**12, 24, 36, or 48 months.** Longer term = lower monthly, more total paid.

## Two plans

1. **Lease-to-Own** — you **own the car** at the end of the term.
2. **Flexi Lease** — lower monthly; at the end you can **pay the buyout to own**,
   **return** the car, or **re-lease**.

## Ownership transfer

For Lease-to-Own, ownership transfers to your name once **all instalments are
paid** and the underlying **bank mortgage on the vehicle is cleared**. RTA
transfer fees are paid by the customer.

## Mileage

A fair-use mileage cap applies (stated in your Agreement). Excess kilometres are
charged at the per-km rate in your Agreement.

## Maintenance

Service the vehicle only at **Prime Hire-approved workshops**. Neglected
maintenance or unauthorised modification may void cover and breach the Agreement.

## Late & missed payments

A late fee applies after the grace period. Repeated default may lead to
suspension of use and repossession, per UAE law. Title does not pass until all
amounts are settled.

## Early settlement

You may settle early by paying the outstanding principal plus the early
settlement fee stated in your Agreement.

## Cancellation / cooling-off

Any cooling-off rights are as set out in your Agreement and applicable UAE law.

## Governing law

UAE law, Emirate of Dubai; Dubai Courts have jurisdiction.
