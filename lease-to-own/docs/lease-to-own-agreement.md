## 3. Payments

1. The Lessee pays the **down payment** and **first monthly payment** plus the
   **refundable security deposit** before vehicle handover.
2. Monthly payments are due on the stated date by direct debit / bank transfer.
3. **Late payment:** a fee of AED ____ applies after a grace period of ____ days.
4. All payments are inclusive of 5% VAT on the lease service component.

## 4. What is included

- Comprehensive motor insurance.
- Scheduled service and routine maintenance (up to the annual allowance).
- Annual RTA registration and inspection renewal.

**Not included:** Salik/tolls, traffic fines, fuel, Darb tolls, parking, and any
damage beyond fair wear and tear. Fines incurred during the lease are the
Lessee's responsibility and will be recharged.

## 5. Use of the Vehicle

- UAE roads only; no off-road, racing, sub-leasing, or commercial taxi/limousine
  use without written consent.
- Mileage cap: **______ km/month**. Excess at **AED ____ /km**.
- The Lessee keeps the vehicle serviced at Company-approved workshops.

## 6. Ownership & End of Term

- **Lease-to-Own:** upon payment of all monthly instalments and clearance of the
  underlying bank mortgage, the Company transfers the vehicle to the Lessee's
  name. Transfer/RTA fees are borne by the Lessee.
- **Flexi Lease:** at term end the Lessee may (a) pay the **buyout** to own,
  (b) **return** the vehicle in agreed condition, or (c) **re-lease** a vehicle.

## 7. Default & Repossession

If the Lessee misses **____ consecutive payments**, the Company may suspend use
and repossess the vehicle after written notice, in accordance with UAE law.
Title does not pass until all sums are paid in full.

## 8. Insurance & Liability

The vehicle is insured comprehensively. The Lessee is liable for the insurance
excess/deductible on any at-fault claim and for losses voided by the policy
(e.g. driving under the influence, unauthorised driver).

## 9. Termination by Lessee

Early settlement is permitted: the Lessee pays the **remaining principal** plus
an early-settlement fee of ____ % of the outstanding balance. The security
deposit is refunded after deduction of fines, damages and dues.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates and the
Emirate of Dubai. Disputes are subject to the Dubai Courts.
