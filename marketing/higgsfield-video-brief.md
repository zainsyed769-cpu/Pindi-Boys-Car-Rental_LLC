## ⭐ Recommended workflow (read this first)

**Use Image-to-Video, not pure Text-to-Video.**
Text-to-video will invent generic cars and fake number plates. Instead:

1. Take 6–8 clean photos of your *actual* cars (Patrol, a luxury sedan, an MG economy car, the logo on the office).
2. In Higgsfield choose **Image → Video**, upload each photo, then apply the **motion preset** listed per clip below and paste the prompt as the motion description.
3. Generate at **5 seconds** per clip. Make a **16:9** set (YouTube/website) and a **9:16** set (Instagram Reels / TikTok / WhatsApp Status).
4. Stitch in the order below; add the voiceover + music; burn in the on-screen captions.

If you have no photos yet, use the text prompts as-is — they're written to be cinematic and generic-safe.

**Global style line — append to every prompt:**
> *cinematic, ultra-realistic, golden-hour Dubai light, polished reflective car paint, shallow depth of field, 35mm, high dynamic range, no text artifacts, no distorted logos*
